# Research and content

## Planning path

1. Define the research question and reader's use
2. Establish source and evidence tiers
3. Choose the analytical or production method
4. Review, publish and retain traceable citations

## Required coverage

| area | label | question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| source_provenance | Source credibility | Are sources primary, authoritative and traceable? |
| research_content_method | Method | How do the materials support the conclusion or output? |
| review_approval | Review and approval | Who checks facts, reasoning and risks? |
| publication_distribution | Publication and distribution | How does the work reach and serve its readers? |

## Dependency rules

| rule_id | statement | direction_hint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| draft-after-sources | Conclusions or content need traceable sources and a clear method. | draft depends_on sources and method |
| publication-after-review | Release requires sources, method and completed review. | publication depends_on review |

## Completion evidence

- Traceable sources
- Method description
- Review record

## Closeout checks

- Conclusions stay within evidence
- References and limitations are visible

## Common mistakes

- Repeating layers of secondary summaries
- Many citations without method
- Treating completion as distribution
