# Education

## Planning path

1. Define learner starting point, ability goal and real context
2. Design content, practice, feedback, collaboration and teacher intervention
3. Establish formative and summative assessment and learning evidence
4. Define boundaries for teachers, parents, administrators and learners
5. Verify transfer, continued learning and equitable access

## Required coverage

| area | label | question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| learner_context | Learner context | What are their background, aims, constraints and situations? |
| learning_outcomes | Outcomes | What can they do afterwards, and how do recall, understanding and transfer differ? |
| learning_experience | Learning experience | How do content, practice, feedback and social learning build ability? |
| assessment_evidence | Assessment | What performance proves learning rather than activity? |
| educator_governance | Teaching roles | What can teachers, parents, admins and AI observe, decide or interrupt? |
| access_inclusion | Equity and access | How do people with different abilities, devices, languages and resources participate? |

## Completion evidence

- Goal-to-assessment correspondence
- Real learners' practice, feedback and change
- Role, exception and accessibility checks

## Closeout checks

- Completion or viewing time does not stand in for learning
- Assessment matches the intended ability
- Add the minors overlay where relevant

## Common mistakes

- Stockpiling course material before defining outcomes
- Treating right answers as transfer
- Ignoring teachers and administrators
