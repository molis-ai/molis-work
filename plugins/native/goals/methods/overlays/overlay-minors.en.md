# Minors

## Planning path

1. Identify age, maturity, context and guardians
2. Assess content, interaction, data, spending and compulsive-use risk
3. Design age-appropriate experience, protective defaults and guardian access
4. Provide reporting, escalation, commercial limits and recovery
5. Test understanding, safety and autonomy across ages and guardianship

## Required coverage

| area | label | question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| age_context | Age context | Who may use it, and how do comprehension, judgment and autonomy vary? |
| guardian_role | Guardians | Which actions require awareness, consent, observation or intervention? |
| child_data | Children's data | What should not be collected, inferred, published or used for personalization? |
| content_contact_safety | Contact and content safety | How are inappropriate content, strangers, bullying and inducement prevented and handled? |
| commercial_wellbeing | Wellbeing and commerce | Do payment, rewards, alerts or time exploit vulnerability? |
| age_recovery | Protection and recovery | How do age mistakes, abuse and harm reach escalation and recovery? |

## Dependency rules

| rule_id | statement | direction_hint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| experience-after-age-risk | Interaction, recommendations, social and commerce require age context and risk assessment. | experience depends_on age and safety assessment |
| launch-after-safeguarding | Release for minors needs verified defaults, guardianship, reporting and escalation. | minors launch depends_on safeguarding evidence |

## Completion evidence

- Age tiers, risks and protective defaults
- Guardian, reporting and escalation drill
- Comprehension of key choices at different ages

## Closeout checks

- Defaults protect without making children find a switch
- Commerce and interaction do not exploit judgment differences
- Child protection requirements confirmed separately for the market

## Common mistakes

- Relying on an age field
- Shifting all responsibility to guardians
- Hiding addiction, bullying or spending risk behind engagement
