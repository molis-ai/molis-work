# Content and communication

## Planning path

1. Define audience and moment
2. Identify main claim and evidence
3. Choose channel-specific formats
4. Publish, observe and iterate

## Required coverage

| area | label | question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| audience_moment | Audience and moment | Who sees and acts on this, and when? |
| claim_evidence | Claims and evidence | Why should the audience believe it? |
| distribution_feedback | Distribution and feedback | How will it reach people and how will we know it works? |

## Dependency rules

| rule_id | statement | direction_hint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| claim-after-evidence | Claims need verifiable audience problems and product facts. | claim depends_on audience and product evidence |
| publication-after-review | Release needs fact, channel and risk checks. | publication depends_on reviewed content |

## Completion evidence

- Evidence of the audience's need
- Product or factual evidence
- Distribution feedback

## Closeout checks

- One line describes the distinct mechanism
- The message matches the actual product

## Common mistakes

- Slogans without evidence
- Same line to everyone
- No feedback after release
