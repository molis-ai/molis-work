# Customer success and service

## Planning path

1. Identify customer results, roles and success signals
2. Design onboarding, adoption, expansion and renewal
3. Provide knowledge, training, support, escalation and recovery
4. Connect usage, service history, customer health and feedback
5. Improve using outcomes, support quality, retention and referrals

## Required coverage

| area | label | question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| customer_outcome | Customer outcome | What business or personal result should buying change? |
| stakeholder_roles | Roles | What do buyers, admins, users and service staff own? |
| adoption_lifecycle | Adoption lifecycle | What states lead from onboarding to routine use, expansion and renewal? |
| support_escalation | Support and escalation | How do self-help, agents, specialists and emergencies connect? |
| customer_health | Customer health | Which usage, outcome or relationship signals reveal risk early? |
| feedback_learning | Learning | How do service problems improve the product, content and workflow? |

## Dependency rules

| rule_id | statement | direction_hint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| service-plan-after-outcome | Onboarding, training and support need the customer's goals, roles and actual usage path. | service plan depends_on customer outcome and product flow |
| expansion-after-success | Expansion and renewal rely on delivered value and evidence that risks were handled. | expansion depends_on demonstrated customer success |

## Completion evidence

- Map of outcomes, roles and adoption stages
- Real support and escalation cases
- Health, attainment and renewal signals

## Closeout checks

- Service follows customer outcomes, not internal ticket routing
- Clear escalation and recovery for exceptions
- Feedback traces to product or process decisions

## Common mistakes

- Equating customer success with reactive support
- Measuring response speed without resolution
- Pushing expansion or renewal before value
