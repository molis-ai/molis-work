Use this when the person provides meeting notes, an attachment or a Pages document and asks for minutes people can act on.

1. Read the original. For a document, read its body through `read-capability`. Do not infer the contents from its title or summary.
2. Use three sections: **Decisions** (one sentence for each settled decision); **Action items** (who, what and by when; use “Unspecified” for missing details); **Open questions** (what is undecided and who needs to decide).
3. After each decision and action item, cite the evidence in parentheses using a keyword or timestamp so the person can check it.
4. End with one sentence listing what the source did not specify, such as owners or dates.
5. If asked to write a Pages document, create a new one. Do not overwrite an existing document unless explicitly asked.
