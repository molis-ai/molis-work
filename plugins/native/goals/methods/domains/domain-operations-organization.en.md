# Operations and organizational workflow

## Planning path

1. Define service outcome and trigger
2. Assign roles, permissions and tools
3. Design handoffs and exceptions
4. Separate setting up the capability from operating it
5. Run, record evidence and propose finite improvements

## Required coverage

| area | label | question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| roles_responsibilities | Roles | Who can own each task with the available commitment? |
| permissions | Authority | Who approves which action? |
| tools_workflow | Workflow | Where are facts recorded and handed off? |
| exception_handling | Exceptions | How are blocks, conflicts and failures handled? |
| measurement | Measurement | How are efficiency, quality and outcome observed? |

## Dependency rules

| rule_id | statement | direction_hint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| workflow-after-ownership | Workflow and tooling depend on agreed roles, permissions and actual capacity. | workflow depends_on roles and permissions |
| improvement-after-measurement | Improvement uses real operations, exception records and measured outcomes. | improvement depends_on operational evidence |

## Completion evidence

- Confirmed roles
- Real process walk-through
- Outcome metrics

## Closeout checks

- Runs with available commitments
- Exceptions have an owner and a recovery action
- Recurring operations do not keep the setup Goal open

## Common mistakes

- Copying a large company's process
- Responsibility without capacity
- Records disconnected from work
- Permanent open Goals for recurring work
