# Home open-page

Mode: Operate. High-fidelity slice only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Home is the act of opening and closing one page of work, not a dashboard of work. It refuses a grid of task, calendar, materials and stash competing for attention.

OWN-WORLD: Workbench field `--page` / paper `--paper`, Inter, one accent for the current action and bookmark. One paper object; drawers at the edge; no card grid.

STORY: You sit down, open the page you left, work, then leave a bookmark and close it. Other things have a place without being on stage.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A still-folded paper in the middle of the field. Object title on the paper. Last bookmark line. Primary “打开草稿，接着想”. Quiet “换一件事 · 先放下一些想法”. Optional one-line due hint at the edge. “今天先到这里” at the bottom. No counts.

FORM: Desk page with a bookmark. Motion is unfold / fold / dock, 280ms `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance — slice-only until production is authorized.
