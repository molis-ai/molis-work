# Market analysis

## Planning path

1. Define market boundaries and decision
2. Identify users, alternatives and value chain by layer
3. Compare differences and evidence
4. Offer directions and a verification plan

## Required coverage

| area | label | question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| market_boundary | Market boundary | At what level do alternatives compete to solve what problem? |
| customer_moment | User moment | Who chooses or abandons the current option, and when? |
| alternatives_competition | Alternatives | What are real alternatives and structural differences? |
| strategic_options | Directions | Which choices does this give the current project? |

## Dependency rules

| rule_id | statement | direction_hint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| comparison-after-boundary | Comparisons need agreed market boundaries, user moments and dimensions. | comparison depends_on market boundary |
| recommendation-after-landscape | Recommendations need evidence about the market, users and alternatives. | recommendation depends_on market evidence |

## Completion evidence

- Primary or authoritative market sources
- Real user or product evidence
- Method to test key assumptions

## Closeout checks

- Give options rather than a single arbitrary answer
- State what it means for the project

## Common mistakes

- Substituting a feature grid for analysis
- Treating unexpressed demand as proof against innovation
- Collecting links without a decision
