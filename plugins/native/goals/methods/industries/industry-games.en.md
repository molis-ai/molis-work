# Games

## Planning path

1. Prove playable value with the core loop, intended players and emotion
2. Connect progression, rewards, resources, difficulty and long-term economy
3. Establish production for levels, narrative, art, sound and configuration
4. Complete social, safety, platform, monetization and support boundaries
5. Verify with actual play, retention, economy, content cadence and community feedback

## Required coverage

| area | label | question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| player_experience | Experience | Which players gain what emotion and capability from which loop? |
| progression_economy | Progression and economy | How do reward, resource, difficulty, payment and long-term goals stay healthy? |
| content_pipeline | Content pipeline | How is play, level, narrative and art content produced, checked and released continuously? |
| social_community | Community | How do cooperation, competition, expression, reports and moderation work? |
| platform_commerce | Platform and commerce | How do platform features, saves, purchases, rights and refunds agree? |
| live_operations | Live operations | How do events, updates, support, data and incident response connect? |

## Dependency rules

| rule_id | statement | direction_hint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| content-scale-after-loop | Large content or economy production requires a proven loop, player feedback and tools. | content scale depends_on validated core loop and pipeline |
| live-launch-after-operations | Online/live release needs platform, economy, community safety, support and recovery readiness. | live launch depends_on operational readiness |

## Completion evidence

- Real players' loop and progression behavior
- Content production-to-release example
- Economy, community, platform and incident drill

## Closeout checks

- Use the game-design method as well; operations cannot prove play
- Monetization preserves fun and fairness
- Add minors or UGC overlays where relevant

## Common mistakes

- Scaling content before the loop is proven
- Designing stats without observing play
- Treating release as the end without live or community operations
